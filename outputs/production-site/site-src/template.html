Pricing & ownership

What do we pay and what do we keep?

We start with your software bills and the time your team spends doing the same work twice. You see which change is worth making and how we’ll measure it. Read the FAQs

A share of the savings

Our success fee is an agreed percentage of verified net savings. We agree the scope, rate, measurement period and cost baseline before delivery. We deduct replacement services, running costs and agreed transition costs before calculating our share. You keep the rest.

A retired licence counts when the charge stops. We report time saved separately unless it also reduces what you spend.

The software is yours to run

You own the code we write for your business. We hand over your repository, source code and instructions to deploy and run it. Your team can add the next workflow itself, bring in a developer it trusts, or choose ongoing support from Integral.

We build our software so beginners can use, manage and maintain it. If you don’t have a developer on hand, we’ll set you up and train you to run the whole system yourself, without paying Integral an ongoing maintenance fee.

You can use and modify the reusable foundation indefinitely. Third-party components keep their own licences.

What if the software doesn’t reduce our overheads?

You keep the software for free. We compare your running costs with the baseline we agreed before work began. If your overheads haven’t fallen after we account for the cost of the change, you pay no success fee.

The initial review is free. If we find no significant savings, you keep the analysis and workflow maps. If verified net savings are zero or negative, the success fee is zero. We agree any hosting or third-party costs with you before work begins.

What would it take to use this with our team?

The demo uses sample records. Before rollout, we agree which systems to connect, who needs access and how your team will test the workflow. We also agree who will run and support it.

Frequently asked questions

Your data

How do we keep our data if we leave?

Your CRM stays in your account unless you choose to move it. If we build you a database, we agree where it lives, who holds the admin access and how your team can export and restore it. You keep the code we write for you.

Which emails and systems can Integral check?

You choose which systems, mailboxes and dates to include. A check can compare a direct number in an email signature with the number in your CRM. If they differ, your team can read the message before changing the contact. Try it with a sample incoming email.

How do I know an email or phone number is correct?

Open the contact record. Each detail has its own last check, last verification and source. For a direct line, a useful note is “Sam answered and confirmed this is his desk number.” If nobody has verified the number, you can see that too. See a record with dated checks.

Does a delivery receipt verify the email address?

A delivery receipt shows that the receiving mail server accepted that message. If Sam replies that this is the best address for him, you can save his reply as evidence for that address.

When does contact data get old?

A job change, a bounced email or a reassigned number can make yesterday’s details wrong. Check again when that happens. For an active account, a handover or renewal is a good time to confirm the details. For a dormant lead, check before the next outreach.

Software and savings

How can you cut our CRM bill?

We compare the seats and add-ons on your invoice with what each team uses. That can mean dropping unused seats, moving occasional users to a cheaper plan or retiring a separate reporting tool. A saving counts when the charge comes off your bill.

Do we have to replace our existing software?

You can keep the tools that earn their place. If Sales needs the CRM and Finance needs the accounts package, we can connect the two so nobody has to enter the same order twice.

What work can you automate?

Take an order change. A customer asks for two extra places. The system can show the new price, send the request to the person who approves it and give Finance the adjustment to review. The team enters the change once.

What can an AI receptionist take off our plate?

It can answer routine enquiries, check a diary and hand calls to a person. If nobody is available, it can leave the team a callback with the caller’s details and preferred time. To compare costs, use the same call volume and include phone charges, voice processing and human cover. Explore AI reception.

What happens if a buyer replies before the next follow-up?

Their reply goes to the rep who owns the deal. The workflow can pause queued reminders across channels while the rep agrees the next step. A booking closes the prospecting chase, and a request to stop updates the contact’s preferences. Explore sales follow-ups.

How do you prove we’ve saved money?

We agree the starting cost and the period to compare. For example, removing £600 a month in licences and spending £150 a month to run the replacement saves £450 a month before any agreed transition costs. We report hours saved separately. Your fee is a share of verified net savings.
