Privacy notice

Updated 24 November 2026

Integral decides how to use personal information for this website, business enquiries and our own outreach. For questions, requests or complaints, contact Integral’s founder, Ben Webster, at ben@byintegral.com or call +44 7417 373688.

To stop marketing messages

You can object at any time. Reply “stop” to the message or email ben@byintegral.com. We’ll stop using your details for direct marketing and keep the minimum information needed to respect your choice.

When we contact someone at work

We look for companies with a specific workflow where our services may be relevant. We may record the company, your name and role, published work contact details, where and when we found them, why the business appears relevant, and any contact history, reply or objection.

Sources include your company website, public professional profiles, Companies House and published accounts of the work your company does. We may use search services and check a published work address with Hunter for signs that it can receive email. If we contacted you, you can ask Ben which source we used for your details.

We rely on our legitimate interest in finding business customers and introducing relevant services to people responsible for that work. We consider relevance, reasonable expectations and the impact on you before contacting you. For marketing emails to sole traders and certain partnerships, we need consent or a valid existing-customer exception. When we obtain details elsewhere, we provide this notice by our first contact and within one month of collecting them, unless a legal exception applies.

When you get in touch

Emails and calls may give us your contact details, organisation, message and any information you choose to share. We use them to reply, discuss your requirements and manage our relationship. Our basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries and providing our services, or taking steps you request towards a contract with you. We may also retain records where the law requires it. If your email app loads our signature logo, it requests the same image used for every recipient. The image URL contains no individual tracking code.

Website visits, cookies and demos

  • Hosting. Cloudflare Pages delivers the site. Requests can include an IP address, requested URL, date and time, and browser information. Our legitimate interest is delivering the site reliably and keeping it secure. The site’s own code sets no analytics or advertising cookies and loads no marketing trackers.
  • Browser storage. Appearance preferences and a training plan you choose to save use local storage on this device. Some CRM practice edits are kept in session storage so they survive reloading the same tab. Clear this site’s data in your browser settings to remove stored items. The demos use fictional records and simulate calls, email and payments. Please use sample details when trying them.
  • Downloads. Discovery briefs, walkthrough summaries and training-plan downloads are generated in your browser. They stay on your device unless you choose to share them.
  • Booking preview. The current timetable lets you check dates and enter an email, phone number and optional notes. These entries stay in the open dialog and are cleared when you close it. Checking them in preview sends nothing to Integral or a booking provider and makes no appointment.
  • External links. Following a link to another service, such as LinkedIn, takes you to a site with its own privacy practices.

Who handles information and where

Cloudflare hosts the website. Porkbun is our domain registrar. Mailpool is our business mailbox provider. When an email passes through that mailbox, Mailpool processes the sender and recipient addresses, message content and any attachments to send, receive and store the message. Hunter may process a published business address we check.

These providers may use other suppliers and process information outside the UK, including in the US. Cloudflare’s terms describe transfer arrangements including standard contractual clauses with the UK Addendum where applicable. Contact Ben for details of the safeguards relevant to your information.

How long information is kept

We keep prospect research while the company and role are relevant to an active opportunity. We remove or update details when we learn that someone has moved, a source is out of date or the opportunity is closed. Enquiry and client correspondence is kept while needed to handle the request or relationship and to meet legal obligations or deal with claims. An opt-out record is kept for as long as needed to prevent further marketing.

Your rights and complaints

You can ask for a copy of your information, for corrections, or, where the conditions apply, deletion, restriction or a portable copy. You can object to processing based on legitimate interests and withdraw any consent you gave. Your right to stop direct marketing is absolute.

Write to ben@byintegral.com to exercise a right or raise a concern. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office.
