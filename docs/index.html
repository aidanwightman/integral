Pricing & ownership

What do we pay and what do we keep?

A lower software bill is one place to start. We review how work gets done across your business. Where is someone entering the same order twice? Which approvals hold up an invoice? We use your records to show where you’re losing time or money and which fix is worth making first.

A share of the savings

Our success fee is an agreed percentage of verified net savings. We agree the scope, rate, measurement period and cost baseline before delivery. We deduct replacement services, running costs and agreed transition costs before calculating our share. You keep the rest.

A retired licence counts when the charge stops. We report time saved separately unless it also reduces what you spend.

The software is yours to run

You own the code we write for your business. We hand over your repository, source code and instructions to deploy and run it. Your team can add the next workflow itself, bring in a developer it trusts, or choose ongoing support from Integral.

You can use and modify the reusable foundation indefinitely. Third-party components keep their own licences.

What if you find no significant savings?

You keep the analysis and workflow maps we’ve completed, free. If a project goes ahead and verified net savings are zero or negative, the success fee is zero. Any hosting or third-party costs are agreed before work begins.

Can we keep some of our existing tools?

Yes. The design starts with your requirements. The agreed solution may connect existing services, replace selected workflows, or build a broader workspace. We assess integrations, data access and contractual constraints as part of discovery.

Is everything in the demo ready for production?

The demo uses synthetic data to show designs and workflows. Your production scope is agreed separately, including integrations, identity, permissions, migration, testing and support. The walkthrough distinguishes working local actions from simulated external services.
